# Trademark Policy

"Moatkeeper" and the Moatkeeper logo identify the official Moatkeeper app and
project. The code is free software under the GPL (see
[LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)); this policy covers only the name and marks.

## Why this exists

Moatkeeper holds credentials for people's firewalls. A modified build
distributed under the Moatkeeper name could quietly send those credentials
somewhere else, or weaken how it checks certificates. Users must be able to
trust that anything called "Moatkeeper" is the genuine, verifiable build.

## What you may do without asking

- Refer to Moatkeeper by name to describe it, discuss it, review it, or say
  your project is compatible with or based on it ("a fork of Moatkeeper").
- Use the name in unmodified redistributions of official releases, with their
  original signatures intact.

## What requires written permission

- Distributing a modified version under the name "Moatkeeper" or any
  confusingly similar name, or with the Moatkeeper logo or icon.
- Using the name or logo in an app store listing, domain name, or account name
  for anything other than the official project.
- Implying endorsement by or affiliation with the Moatkeeper project.

## Forks

Forks are welcome under the GPL. Give yours a distinct name and icon, and make
it clear it is not the official Moatkeeper app.

## Official builds

Only builds signed with the project's published keys are official.
Signing-certificate fingerprints and reproducible-build hashes will be listed
in [README.md](README.md) once releases begin.

## Other projects' marks

"pfSense" and "Netgate" are trademarks of Rubicon Communications, LLC
(Netgate). Moatkeeper uses them only to say what it works with. Moatkeeper is
not affiliated with or endorsed by Netgate.

## Reporting misuse

Report impersonating apps or listings through GitHub (open an issue, or use a
private security advisory if the copy appears malicious).
